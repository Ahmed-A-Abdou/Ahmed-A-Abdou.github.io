NASA Space Apps Challenge Mentor Hackathon Certificate

Galactic Local Mentor

The Event
The 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge, Shebeen El-Kom: a two-day hackathon where teams build solutions to challenges set by NASA and its partner space agencies. I was invited back as a mentor a month after hosting the event's game development session, and was recognised as a Galactic Local Mentor.
What I Helped With
  • Designing the game: turning a challenge into a game idea.
  • Making it actually entertaining: what makes a game fun to play, not just a challenge with a game around it.
  • Developing mechanics: how to build the specific mechanics each team's game needed.
  • Writing the GDD: getting the design onto paper so the whole team builds the same game.
  • Managing scope: cutting the plan down to what a team can actually finish before the deadline, and deciding what to build first.
  • Choosing and using the technology: web-based games, Three.js, and Unity, picking what fits each team's skills and the time they had.
What I Took Away
Scope is where most teams struggle under a deadline. The most useful thing I could do was help them decide early what to leave out, so they finished something playable instead of half of something bigger.
