Unity 6 C# Modular Framework In Development

MilSim: Military Simulation

A framework of military systems, built one system at a time. Each system stands on its own, in its own assembly, and they work together without knowing about each other: the air defence engages anything that says I'm a target, whether it's a drone or a shell from a howitzer it has never heard of. New behaviour arrives as a new asset or a small new class, never as an edit to what already works.

Built entirely from public information, in Unity 6 with URP. The shared building blocks are released as Emplacement, an open-source Unity package.
